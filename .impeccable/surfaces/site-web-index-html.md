# Landing (trailer.madfam.io)

Scope: the one public page, Spanish at `/` and English at `/en`. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: MADFAM's product teams, at a desk, with their own product's public page in mind. They
must believe a film of their page is one command away and that what comes out is real. Action: copy the
command that films a product, then open the repository. Proof on hand: one real take of enclii.dev
(clip and stills under `site/web/footage/`), the director's real shot durations, the export matrix,
the catalog count. Constraints: no invented metrics, no film implied beyond the one that exists, the
pointer character stated with its limits (cinematic takes only, no full take filmed with it), WCAG 2.2 AA, no third-party request.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the glass case outside a neighbourhood cinema, and the cards in it are real stills
of a film the tool made. It refuses the developer-tool page (headline, install line, feature grid) and
the dark letterboxed screening room.

OWN-WORLD: Flat printed ink at page scale: a vermilion field, then marigold, night blue and green
fields, each owning a whole band. Lobby cards on pale stock with a wide ink border, pinned at small
angles, soft pinned-paper shadows. Title lettering in a fat poster face with an off-register second
ink; condensed billing-block capitals for credits; a brush script for "presenta". Halftone dots over
stills. Controls are ticket stubs with a perforated edge.

STORY: The visitor sees a real film playing in a lobby card, understands it was made from a live page
by a command, reads the five shots as five cards that play when chosen, learns what else runs today and
what the pointer character does and does not do yet, then copies the command for their own product.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Vermilion field edge to edge. Left: the headline in poster lettering at display scale,
two lines of plain copy, and the ticket stub holding the produce command with its copy control, the
primary action. Right, larger than the copy column: one oversized lobby card, tilted, holding the
playing clip of the Enclii take, with the title band and the billing block of real credits under it. On
a phone the card comes first under the headline and the ticket follows.

FORM: lobby cards and marquee bills of Mexican neighbourhood cinemas, position 4 of 7 on the ordered
list, seed:db9e953db5ea0316. Raise kept from the call sheet: each card carries its shot number and what
the camera does. Signature interaction: choosing a card plays that shot inside the card, then it
settles back to its still. Motion grammar: print registering (the second ink slides into register
once, on arrival); nothing else animates on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: indexing stays off until the owner says otherwise.
